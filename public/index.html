ML Recommendations

Use − for not interested, → for next read, and + to like. Refresh shows a loading state while new picks are generated.

ML score is a match rating from 0% to 100%. Higher means the model thinks you are more likely to enjoy the book.

It blends two signals from your likes:

  • 60% content similarity — themes, genres, and writing style (TF-IDF)
  • 40% collaborative filtering — books enjoyed by readers with similar taste

The percentage on each card is that combined score. It helps compare recommendations to each other, not predict a perfect read.
